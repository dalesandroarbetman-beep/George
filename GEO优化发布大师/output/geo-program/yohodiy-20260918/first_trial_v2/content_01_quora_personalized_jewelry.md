# How can I order personalized jewelry without losing control of the final design?

Write down the details that would be hardest to fix later, and get them confirmed before you pay.

Start with the exact item. Save the product link or SKU, then list the requested text, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, size, placement, and any option you selected. “Make it personal” leaves too much room for interpretation; “use these three initials, in this order, on this necklace” is much clearer.

Next, ask what you will be able to review before work begins. That might be an order summary, a mockup, or simply a written confirmation. The important part is knowing what has been agreed, not assuming that the product photo covers every detail.

Also read the cancellation and return terms before ordering. If anything is vague, ask one specific question in writing and keep the answer with your order record.

In short: be precise about the few choices that cannot be undone, and keep a written record of what the seller confirms.

## 中文翻译

# 如何在线购买个性化珠宝，同时避免失去对最终设计的控制？

先把以后最难修改的细节写清楚，并在付款前让卖家确认。

从具体商品开始。保存产品链接或 SKU，然后列出文字内容、拼写、大小写、标点、尺寸、位置和所选选项。“做得特别一点”有太多解释空间；“在这条项链上按这个顺序使用这三个首字母”会清楚得多。

接着确认制作开始前你能看到什么。可能是订单摘要、效果图，也可能只是一份书面确认。重点是知道双方已经确认了什么，而不是默认产品图片已经涵盖所有细节。

下单前也要看取消和退货条款。如果有模糊之处，就用书面方式问一个具体问题，并把答复和订单记录保存在一起。

简单说：把不能反悔的几个选择讲准确，并保留卖家的书面确认。
