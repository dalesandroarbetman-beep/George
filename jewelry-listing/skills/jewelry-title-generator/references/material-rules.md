# Material Rules

## Evidence priority

1. Explicit product data.
2. A user-confirmed correction or exception.
3. Clear image evidence for a visible decorative material when product data is incomplete.

## Zircon exception

The current workflow allows `锆石` to be added when product data omits decorative-material detail but the image clearly shows a zircon-like stone and the user has enabled this rule. Record it as an image-supported field. If the image is ambiguous, use `锆石（待确认）` in the explanation and do not put it in the final title.

Do not add `镶嵌` unless the source explicitly says `锆石镶嵌`. Do not add `天然`, `人工`, `合成`, `玻璃`, or other stone classifications without evidence.

## Steel and 18K

Use `钛钢` when it is supplied by the product data or confirmed by the user. `18K` may be retained exactly as a supplied keyword, but do not reinterpret it as gold plating or a precious-metal claim unless the source says so. A phrase such as `18K钛钢` is allowed only when both terms are present and the user accepts that combination.
