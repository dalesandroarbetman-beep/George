# Platform Playbook

Use these as starting heuristics, then adapt them to the video's actual audience, account history, and local timezone. Do not present them as guaranteed algorithm rules.

## TikTok

- Open with the strongest visual or question in the first line.
- Use a direct interaction prompt, such as choosing a color, finish, or styling option.
- Prefer a concise, conversational caption and 5–10 tags mixing brand, category, and platform terms.
- Suggest a cover with the product and the hook text still readable on a small screen.

## Instagram Reels

- Emphasize a save-worthy use case: “Save this for …”.
- Add a color or outfit pairing angle when the footage supports it.
- Suggest a Story poll or question sticker as a follow-up, not as a fabricated feature of the Reel.
- Keep the visual language cohesive with the account grid.

## YouTube Shorts

- Put the click hook in the first words of the title.
- Include honest search phrases such as `how to style`, `DIY`, or `behind the scenes` only when the video supports them.
- Write a description that gives context without keyword stuffing.
- Recommend a cover frame with a clear product state and readable title text.

## Facebook

- Favor a checklist, gifting, or community discussion angle.
- For a boost suggestion, state the audience and small-test assumption; never imply that paid promotion has been run.
- Use numbered choices for comments or polls when they make the decision easy.
- Separate B2B warehouse or wholesale footage from C-end gifting and styling footage.

## X (Twitter)

- Keep the main post at or below 280 characters after rendering the final text.
- Use one short reversal, observation, or quotable line rather than an ad paragraph.
- Reserve extra context for a reply or quote-post plan.
- Check that any link and hashtag count still fit the character limit.

## DIY account

- Reframe toward method: selecting materials, combining components, assembly, or a making decision.
- Keep DIY education separate from the main account's C-end selling copy.
- Do not claim a tutorial step that is not visible or provided by the user.

## Timing heuristic

When no account analytics are available:

- C-end emotional, display, or unboxing content: test evening and weekend windows.
- B-end wholesale, warehouse, or talking-head content: test weekday morning or office windows.
- Stagger the same video's releases across platforms instead of publishing simultaneously everywhere.

Always name the assumed timezone and explain that account analytics should override these starting points.
