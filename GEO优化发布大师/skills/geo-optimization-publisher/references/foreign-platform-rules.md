# Foreign platform adaptation rules

These are working heuristics, not guarantees or official ranking rules. Check the target community's current rules before manual publication.

## Reddit

- Answer the community's question before mentioning the product.
- Use first-hand experience only when the user actually supplied it; otherwise write as a company or practitioner, not a fake customer.
- Disclose affiliation when relevant. Keep one natural CTA and avoid link dumping.
- Preserve caveats, trade-offs, and comments that invite discussion.

## Quora

- Lead with a direct answer, then explain criteria, steps, evidence, and limitations.
- Use headings and concise examples. A product mention must be relevant to the answer and clearly attributable.
- Do not manufacture credentials, testimonials, or “independent” recommendations.

## Indie Hackers

- Prefer build logs, experiments, distribution lessons, failures, metrics with dates, and decisions.
- Separate observed results from hypotheses. Do not turn a launch announcement into a fake retrospective.
- Keep the product link secondary to the useful lesson.

## X

- One claim or question per post; use a short hook and a reply thread only when needed.
- Avoid unsupported virality, ranking, or conversion promises.

## LinkedIn

- Use professional context, decision criteria, process, and evidence; avoid corporate filler.
- Make the author/organization relationship clear.

## Facebook

- Use accessible language, scenario-led framing, and a single discussion prompt.
- Groups may have stricter self-promotion rules; mark group-specific checks as pending until reviewed.
